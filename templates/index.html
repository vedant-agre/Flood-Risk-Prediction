The 7 Steps of Data Science Applied to Flood Risk Assessment

This project rigorously executes the 7 foundational stages of the Data Science Life Cycle on a benchmark dataset of 50,000 meteorological and environmental records.

Step 1

Problem Definition & Domain Understanding

Floods cause extensive casualties, infrastructure devastation, and billions in economic damages annually. The objective is to formulate a multi-class predictive risk classification framework (Low, Moderate, High, Severe) that maps multi-factor rainfall and watershed indicators to operational disaster response thresholds.

Step 2

Data Collection, Multi-Source Integration & Merging

Simulated realistic institutional data acquisition across 3 separate entities: Meteorological Telemetry, Geospatial Environmental Survey, and Civil Demographics. Executed relational inner joins using primary key basin_id with strict 1-to-1 cardinality validation, achieving 100% lossless integration across all 50,000 geographical catchment basins.

Step 3

Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

Performed missing value verification (0 nulls), duplicate removal (0 duplicates), type auditing, and Interquartile Range (IQR) anomaly profiling. Evaluated extreme readings as valid natural meteorological events.

Step 4

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Generated univariate distributions, correlation matrices, target correlation hierarchies, and bivariate interaction boxplots across risk classes. All figures exported at 300 DPI to reports/figures/.

Step 5

Feature Engineering & Selection

Formulated domain-specific indicators: Hydrological Stress Ratio, Environmental Degradation Index, Infrastructure Resilience Index, and Human Exposure Index. Performed feature scaling and 80/20 stratified partitioning. Conducted a feature leakage audit to eliminate trivial linear sum dependencies.

Step 6

Model Building & Evaluation

Benchmarked 6 algorithms across distinct algorithmic families using 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation: Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Gaussian Naive Bayes, and K-Nearest Neighbors. Evaluated via Accuracy, F1-Macro, Precision, Recall, Confusion Matrices, and Multi-Class ROC-AUC.

Step 7

Data Insights, Deployment & Reporting

Deployed an operational Decision Support Web Dashboard, serialized model pipelines, generated a complete course project report, and built an end-to-end reproducible Jupyter Notebook.
